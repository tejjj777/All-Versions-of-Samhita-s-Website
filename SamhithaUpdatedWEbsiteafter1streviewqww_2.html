About Me

I didn't start in fine dining. I started as a cashier at Market Basket, worked the drive-through at Panera, and learned how a dining room actually runs as a host and expeditor at Rail Trail Flatbread. Those jobs taught me that a good shift comes down to preparation. — knowing the menu, prioritizing the guests, and being ready before the rush.

Since starting school in Chicago, I've staged at Frontera Grill and Sifr and volunteered with the National Restaurant Association, which pushed me to be faster and more precise. My coursework covers the business side of food — ethics, nutrition, how the industry works — and I bring both halves of that with me on every shift.
